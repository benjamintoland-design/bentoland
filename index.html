About

No photographer manifesto.

Ben Toland · North Ogden, Utah

I take pictures because I like taking pictures. Birds, landscapes, architecture, family trips, strange light — whatever makes me stop and look twice.

This site is a personal home for the photographs I want to keep around, not a storefront and not a content machine. New work goes up front; older favorites live in the archive.
